Busyfox Marketing LLP · Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, India · Last updated 15 November 2026

In short: you choose to share your Instagram account's numbers with us so we can audit your account and plan your growth. We only read them. We never see your password, never post as you and never read your messages.

Who we are

Creator Connect is run by Busyfox Marketing LLP ("Busy Fox", "we"), a marketing agency in Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, India. For the information you share here, we are the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we receive

When you tap Continue with Instagram and approve, Instagram gives us read-only access to your professional account:

  • your profile basics: username, name, profile photo, account type, follower, following and post counts;
  • your posts, reels and stories, with their captions, dates, likes, comments, views, reach, saves and shares;
  • account insights: reach, views, profile activity, follows and unfollows, and when your followers are online;
  • audience insights in groups only: age ranges, gender, cities and countries. We do not receive a list of your followers.

We also keep the answers you give us on the "what do you enjoy making" form.

We do not receive your password, your direct messages, or any way to post, comment, like or change anything on your account.

Why we use it

Only to prepare your Instagram audit, growth plan and media kit, to track your progress while we work with you, and to talk to you about them. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to target you.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Your access key and saved numbers are stored on Cloudflare's servers, which may be outside India. Only the Busy Fox team working on your account can read them. Your report is shared with you through a private link; anyone you forward that link to can open it.

How long we keep it

Instagram's access lasts 60 days and we renew it while we work with you. When you remove access or ask us to, we delete your access key, saved stories, snapshots and form answers from our systems within 30 days. Reports already sent to you stay with you.

How to stop sharing

  • In Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → find Busy Fox → Remove. Instagram tells us, and we delete your data automatically.
  • Or message us, and we will remove it for you.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, name someone to act for you, and raise a complaint. Write to us using the contact below; we reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
