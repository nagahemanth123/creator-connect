questions

Quick answers.

Do I need a special kind of account?

Yes, a Creator or Business account. It is free to switch: Instagram → Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account.

Why do I need an invitation?

Instagram is still reviewing our app, so for now only invited accounts can connect. The Busy Fox team sends you the invitation. Accepting it is one tap and changes nothing on your profile.

How many followers do I need?

There is no minimum. Instagram shares audience details such as ages and cities only for accounts with 100 followers or more.

Will my followers see anything?

No. Nothing is posted and nothing shows on your profile.

What do you want in return?

Nothing. If you like the report, we would love to hear it. If you ever want paid help with content, you know where we are.
