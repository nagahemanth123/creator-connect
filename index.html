Three quick steps

  1. Use a Creator or Business account. Instagram → Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account. It's free.
  2. Accept our invite. Busy Fox has sent an invite to your Instagram. Open Apps and websites, go to Tester invites and tap Accept. Open my invites
  3. Tap Continue with Instagram, check it's your account, and tap Allow.

You can remove access any time in Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites. Privacy notice
