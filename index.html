questions

Good to know.

Is this safe?

Yes. You approve on Instagram's own page, not ours, and Instagram gives us read-only access. We can't post, comment, like, follow or read your messages, and we never see your password.

Does it cost anything?

No. The report is free. If you'd like Busy Fox to help you grow after that, we'll talk about it on WhatsApp. No pressure.

What do I need?

A Creator or Business Instagram account (switching is free in Settings). Audience details like age and cities show once you have 100 or more followers.

Why does Instagram ask me to accept an invite?

Our Instagram app is new, so for now Instagram asks each creator to accept a short tester invite before connecting. It takes one tap on Instagram's website.

Who sees my numbers?

Only the Busy Fox team working on your report. We share the report with you through a private link. We never sell your data or show it to brands without your yes.

What if I change my mind?

Remove Busy Fox in Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites, or message us. We delete your saved data automatically. Read our privacy notice.
